# A1 identity evidence matrix (issue #215, Q-005 follow-up)

Purpose: per-item attribute evidence from the pinned source records for the
A1 items removed or rejected under the literal-wording standard (ANSWER
Q-000 and Q-003), so the adopted material-attribute policy can be applied to
documented evidence instead of title wording alone. All evidence below is
read directly from the pinned bulk files (SR Legacy 2018-04 and Foundation
2026-10-29). Nothing here changes any adjudication by itself.

Reading guide: "Supported" means the material food identity is supported by
record evidence and a documented naming convention, with no material
contradiction. The mere absence of a variant qualifier on a sibling is NOT
proof of the opposite processing state. "Gap" means evidence remains
insufficient or a material attribute actually conflicts. A generic food-class
match is not proof of a specific package, production history, or formulation.

## Items removed in review round 1 (were staged at the first head)

### lemon-juice-fresh-ready-to-drink -> SR Legacy 167747 "Lemon juice, raw"
- Product and form: lemon, juice. Category: Fruits and Fruit Juices.
- Processing state: "raw" is the dataset's state word for the unprocessed
  form. Every processed sibling carries its qualifier explicitly: 167748
  "Lemon juice from concentrate, canned or bottled", 167801 and 167802
  branded bottled concentrates. 167747 is the unqualified raw lemon-juice
  option among these relevant siblings; the word "raw" does not prove
  fresh-squeezed packaging, lack of pasteurization, or a specific brand.
- Contradictions: none found. "Ready-to-drink" describes usable liquid,
  not whether the source started as concentrate. Scope is generic fresh
  juice composition by weight, not a packaging or pasteurization guarantee.
- Verdict under the adopted material-attribute rubric: SUPPORTED EQUIVALENT.

### lime-juice-fresh-ready-to-drink -> SR Legacy 168156 "Lime juice, raw"
- Same relevant contrast as lemon: sibling 168157 is "Lime juice, canned or
  bottled, unsweetened"; 168156 is the unqualified raw juice record. Raw
  does not independently prove extraction, packaging or pasteurization.
- Contradictions: none shown for a generic fresh-juice composition.
- Verdict: SUPPORTED EQUIVALENT, limited to food identity and grams.

### soy-sauce-regular-ready-to-use -> SR Legacy 174277 "Soy sauce made from soy and wheat (shoyu)"
- Type: shoyu (soy and wheat), the standard soy sauce type; the tamari
  sibling 174278 is separately described.
- Sodium class: the release marks reduced forms explicitly, 172473
  "shoyu, low sodium" and 172474 "reduced sodium". 174277 is the
  unqualified shoyu, and its sodium value (5490 mg per 100 g) is the
  regular level, roughly double the low sodium class.
- Contradictions: none.
- Verdict: SUPPORTED.

## Previously rejected proposals (ANSWER Q-000), same evidence pass

### orange-juice-fresh-ready-to-drink -> SR Legacy 169098 "Orange juice, raw"
- Identical pattern to lemon and lime: every other orange juice record is
  explicitly canned (169099), chilled from concentrate (169100, 169101,
  169920), or frozen concentrate (169102, 169921-169923). 169098 is the
  only raw record. Its parenthetical "(Includes foods for USDA's Food
  Distribution Program)" is a distribution note, not a processing
  qualifier.
- Contradictions: none.
- Verdict: SUPPORTED (same evidence pattern as lemon and lime).

### cocoa-powder-unsweetened-natural-not-dutch -> SR Legacy 169593 "Cocoa, dry powder, unsweetened"
- Processing: 169594 explicitly says "processed with alkali"; 169593 is
  unqualified. That sibling contrast alone is NOT proof of non-alkalization.
  Independent naming evidence supports the generic equivalence: 21 CFR
  163.112(c)(1) and 163.113 require an alkali-processing declaration in
  cocoa labeling, while Iowa State University Extension documents natural
  cocoa commonly marketed simply as "unsweetened cocoa". Sources:
  https://www.law.cornell.edu/cfr/text/21/163.112 and
  https://www.law.cornell.edu/cfr/text/21/163.113 and
  https://blogs.extension.iastate.edu/answerline/2025/02/04/natural-vs-dutch-process-cocoa/
- Contradictions: none in the record. The source does not analytically
  verify non-alkalization or claim a particular label formulation.
- Verdict: SUPPORTED EQUIVALENT for generic natural unsweetened cocoa,
  using documented nomenclature plus release-specific sibling contrast.

### pomegranate-juice-ready-to-drink -> Foundation 2727588 "Juice, pomegranate, from concentrate, shelf-stable"
- Processing: the Foundation record states "from concentrate" and
  "shelf-stable". Neither term contradicts "ready-to-drink" or inherently
  rules out 100% juice. The target does not require not-from-concentrate.
- Source candidates: SR Legacy 167787 "Pomegranate juice, bottled" also
  exists in the pinned SR release, contrary to the earlier claim that the
  Foundation record was the only candidate. SR 167787 has complete core
  macros but its record name alone does not establish 100% juice content
  or added-sweetener status. Compare both candidates in a later pass.
- Core-nutrient gap: Foundation 2727588 lacks energy, total fat, and total
  carbohydrate rows despite having sugar-component observations. That
  sparse record is not suitable as the main macro basis without an
  explicitly reviewed exception.
- Verdict: GAP / unresolved for verified 100%-juice formulation and a
  usable primary macro basis, NOT an alleged concentrate contradiction.

### salsa-red -> Foundation 746777 "Sauce, salsa, ready-to-serve"
- Composition evidence: the record's inputs are branded chunky medium
  salsas (Tostitos Chunky Medium, Pace Chunky Medium). The target says
  "red", NOT "smooth"; do not invent a smooth-texture requirement.
- The record does not explicitly verify the target's red/tomato identity
  or generalize its branded formulation to generic red salsa. A reviewed
  representative match or recipe-time proxy may become possible if those
  material attributes can be supported independently.
- Verdict: GAP / insufficient generalization evidence, not a proven
  contradiction to an invented smooth target.

### chocolate-chips-semisweet-baking -> SR Legacy 167976 "Candies, semisweet chocolate"
- Form: the record describes semisweet chocolate confection rather than
  chips. Shape alone need not alter per-100-g nutrition, but the record
  does not establish chip formulation or its possible fat/stabilizer
  differences. Household chip-volume conversions cannot be borrowed from
  bar portions.
- Verdict: GAP / composition comparability not yet verified. Retain as a
  candidate or clearly disclosed grams-only recipe-time proxy, not a
  canonical chip profile.
