# A1 identity evidence matrix (issue #215, Q-005 follow-up)

Purpose: per-item attribute evidence from the pinned source records for the
A1 items removed or rejected under the literal-wording standard (ANSWER
Q-000 and Q-003), so the matching standard James chooses can be applied to
documented evidence instead of title wording alone. All evidence below is
read directly from the pinned bulk files (SR Legacy 2018-04 and Foundation
2026-10-29). Nothing here changes any adjudication by itself.

Reading guide: "Supported" means every material attribute of the target is
either stated in the record or established by the dataset's own naming
taxonomy (a qualifier that processed or variant siblings carry explicitly
is absent from the candidate record), with no contradiction. "Gap" means a
material attribute is contradicted by the record or has no evidence.

## Items removed in review round 1 (were staged at the first head)

### lemon-juice-fresh-ready-to-drink -> SR Legacy 167747 "Lemon juice, raw"
- Product and form: lemon, juice. Category: Fruits and Fruit Juices.
- Processing state: "raw" is the dataset's state word for the unprocessed
  form. Every processed sibling carries its qualifier explicitly: 167748
  "Lemon juice from concentrate, canned or bottled", 167801 and 167802
  branded bottled concentrates. 167747 is the only non-concentrate lemon
  juice record in the release.
- Contradictions: none found in the record. "Ready-to-drink" is a
  packaging phrase; the record neither states nor contradicts it.
- Verdict under an attributes rubric: SUPPORTED.

### lime-juice-fresh-ready-to-drink -> SR Legacy 168156 "Lime juice, raw"
- Same pattern as lemon: the only sibling is 168157 "Lime juice, canned or
  bottled, unsweetened". 168156 is the only non-canned lime juice record.
- Contradictions: none.
- Verdict: SUPPORTED.

### soy-sauce-regular-ready-to-use -> SR Legacy 174277 "Soy sauce made from soy and wheat (shoyu)"
- Type: shoyu (soy and wheat), the standard soy sauce type; the tamari
  sibling 174278 is separately described.
- Sodium class: the release marks reduced forms explicitly, 172473
  "shoyu, low sodium" and 172474 "reduced sodium". 174277 is the
  unqualified shoyu, and its sodium value (5490 mg per 100 g) is the
  regular level, roughly double the low sodium class.
- Contradictions: none.
- Verdict: SUPPORTED.

## Previously rejected proposals (ANSWER Q-000), same evidence pass

### orange-juice-fresh-ready-to-drink -> SR Legacy 169098 "Orange juice, raw"
- Identical pattern to lemon and lime: every other orange juice record is
  explicitly canned (169099), chilled from concentrate (169100, 169101,
  169920), or frozen concentrate (169102, 169921-169923). 169098 is the
  only raw record. Its parenthetical "(Includes foods for USDA's Food
  Distribution Program)" is a distribution note, not a processing
  qualifier.
- Contradictions: none.
- Verdict: SUPPORTED (same evidence pattern as lemon and lime).

### cocoa-powder-unsweetened-natural-not-dutch -> SR Legacy 169593 "Cocoa, dry powder, unsweetened"
- Processing: the alkali-processed sibling is explicitly described as such,
  169594 "processed with alkali" (the accepted Dutch entry). 169593 is the
  unqualified record in the same taxonomy, i.e. not alkali processed.
- Contradictions: none; the word "natural" itself does not appear.
- Verdict: SUPPORTED under the unqualified-default pattern (same as soy).

### pomegranate-juice-ready-to-drink -> Foundation 2727588 "Juice, pomegranate, from concentrate, shelf-stable"
- Processing: the record's own description states "from concentrate",
  which contradicts a ready-to-drink (not from concentrate) target. It is
  the only pomegranate juice record in either pinned release.
- Verdict: GAP (explicit contradiction). Stays unresolved under either
  standard.

### salsa-red -> Foundation 746777 "Sauce, salsa, ready-to-serve"
- Composition evidence: the record's input foods are exclusively branded
  chunky medium salsas (Tostitos Chunky Medium, Pace Chunky Medium). A
  generic smooth red salsa identity is not what this composite measures,
  and heat level (medium) and texture (chunky) are material attributes
  for salsa.
- Verdict: GAP (composition contradicts the generic target). Stays
  unresolved under either standard.

### chocolate-chips-semisweet-baking -> SR Legacy 167976 "Candies, semisweet chocolate"
- Form: the record is the candy (bar) form of semisweet chocolate. No
  chip-form semisweet chocolate record exists in SR Legacy. Form is a
  material attribute for a baking ingredient (melt behavior and portion
  weighing differ), and composition alone does not cure it.
- Verdict: GAP (form mismatch, no chip record exists). Stays unresolved
  under either standard.

## Summary

- Supported by documented attributes, no contradictions: lemon juice,
  lime juice, soy sauce, orange juice, cocoa natural (5 items).
- Gaps or contradictions under any standard: pomegranate juice (from
  concentrate), salsa (chunky medium branded composite), chocolate chips
  (no chip-form record) (3 items).
- If the attributes rubric is adopted, A1 becomes 14 imported profiles and
  11 unresolved. If the literal-wording standard stands, A1 remains 9
  imported and 16 unresolved, and this matrix documents why each side
  claims what it claims.
